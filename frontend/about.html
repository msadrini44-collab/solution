Company

Built for the moment when “looks real” is no longer enough.

AntiDeepfake AI is a media-authenticity product for people who need to make careful decisions from imperfect digital evidence: fraud teams, marketplaces, KYC teams, recruiters, newsrooms, investigators, and creators protecting their work.

Our approach is deliberately layered. We do not ask one model to make a magic yes/no decision. The platform compares face integrity, pixel-level artifacts, frequency traces, metadata, liveness cues, audio-visual consistency, temporal behavior, and optional premium provider signals, then reports both the verdict and the evidence behind it.

What we believe

  • Detection should be explainable enough for a human reviewer to challenge it.
  • Borderline results should stay borderline, not be forced into a dramatic label.
  • Uploaded media deserves careful handling, short retention, and clear ownership.
  • Organizations should be able to self-host when privacy or compliance requires it.

Who it is for

AntiDeepfake AI is designed for operational review, fraud screening, content verification, and internal evidence triage. It is not a replacement for legal judgment, expert testimony, or human investigation; it is a fast, documented way to decide what deserves closer review.
