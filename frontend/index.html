How we analyze

Eight core methods, one calibrated verdict

No single technique is reliable on its own. AntiDeepfake runs complementary analyses in parallel and fuses them with a calibrated ensemble — so a borderline result is reported as inconclusive rather than a false accusation.

Face integrity

Facial manipulation analysis

Detects face swaps and reenactment artifacts using a deep classifier applied to aligned facial regions.

Spectral

Frequency-domain inspection

FFT/DCT analysis surfaces the periodic grid patterns generative models leave behind in Fourier space.

Liveness

Presentation & liveness

Eye-state, micro-motion, and skin-texture cues separate live capture from screens, prints, and synthetics.

Temporal

Temporal consistency

Optical-flow analysis across sampled frames flags flicker, warping, and unnatural motion in video.

Audio-visual

Audio-visual sync

Compares lip motion against speech energy and checks the audio spectrum for synthetic-voice signatures.

Provenance

Generator fingerprinting

Attributes likely origin — GAN, diffusion, or genuine capture — from residual noise signatures.

Metadata

Metadata forensics

Inspects EXIF, quantization tables, and editing-tool traces — and flags suspicious metadata stripping.

Pixel-level

Pixel forensics

Error-level analysis, noise-consistency, clone detection, and lighting checks localize edited regions.
