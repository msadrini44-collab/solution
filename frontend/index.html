Eight methods. One clear answer.

No single technique catches everything. AntiDeepfake runs eight independent checks in parallel and combines them into one score — and when a result is borderline, it says so instead of guessing.

Face integrity

Checks for face swaps, reenactment artifacts, and inconsistencies in facial regions.

Frequency analysis

Examines the image in frequency space to find repeating patterns left behind by generators.

Liveness check

Separates live capture from screens, prints, and synthetics using skin texture and motion cues.

Frame consistency

Flags flicker, warping, and unnatural motion across video frames.

Audio-visual sync

Compares lip movement to speech and checks the audio track for synthetic-voice markers.

Source fingerprinting

Identifies whether the image likely came from a camera, a GAN, or a diffusion model.

Metadata review

Inspects EXIF data, compression tables, and editing traces — and flags when metadata has been stripped.

Pixel-level analysis

Error-level, noise-consistency, clone detection, and lighting checks to find edited regions.
